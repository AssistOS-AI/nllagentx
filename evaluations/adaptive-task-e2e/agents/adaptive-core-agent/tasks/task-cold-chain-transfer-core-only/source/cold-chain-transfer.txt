COLD-CHAIN TRANSFER POLICY

Rule CT-1. A refrigerated research sample may be released after a custody transfer only when all of the following are supported for that transfer: the sealed-container identifier is recorded; a temperature reading before handoff and a temperature reading after receipt are both between 2.0 °C and 8.0 °C; the readings come from a thermometer whose calibration is valid at the time of transfer; and both the releasing party and the receiving party acknowledge the transfer.

Rule CT-2. Recording an identifier or reading proves only that the record exists. It does not prove that a thermometer calibration was valid or that an unrecorded acknowledgement occurred.

Rule CT-3. If a temperature is above 8.0 °C for more than five minutes, quarantine is mandatory unless a named stability study explicitly supports the observed temperature and duration. An assertion that the sample is safe is not itself a stability study.
